## 1. Why

Esme's `skill_progress` said silent-e, compound words, Dolch second
grade and multiplication arrays (50 attempts). Cecily answered those.
The planner believed the rows and served her `compare_2digit` (17/36)
and `add_within_100` (11/27) for a month. The 09-14 handoff's "Esme is
not behind" was wrong for the same reason.

And math's lowest skill was `counting.to_20`; reading's two roots
assume a decoder. Nothing was picture-only; the narrator waited 4.5s
"so a reader sees the prompt first."

### The Level 0 module `55d4621`
`lib/level0/` in the birds/gems/music shape: a pre-skill catalog with
prerequisites (`curriculum.ts`), mastery as a pure function of
attempt rows (85% of the last 20, on ≥3 days), `offerable()` that
widens and never takes away, `readyForLevelOne()`. Rounds are
null-item attempts, `response.source` ∈ ladybug / basket / signature,
`response.preSkill`. **No skill rows, no Elo, no `skill_progress`
writes.** A shared MIXED seeded RNG (`rng.ts`) — the raw xorshift gave
near-zero first outputs for small seeds, and every basket round under
seed 400 put one carrot on the left. A distribution test guards it.

### The Ladybug Count `55d4621` — `/level0/ladybug`
Count (tap each, it says its number and flies), glance (dice pattern,
just look), numeral ("tap the seven"). Server regrows the leaf from

numbered row and the voice counts them, computed. Rotates by leaves
*finished*, not taps. Invitation: a small leaf with three ladybugs
beside the letterbox, Level 0 only.

### Bunny's Basket `cdaa170` — `/level0/basket`

basket: carrots lift into two numbered rows. The barn's bunny is now
a reusable `<g>` (`BunnyFigure`). Invitation in Bunny Glade at
(440, 700), Level 0 only.

## 6. Rules added

- **0 is falsy.** `?? 2` keeps a Level 0; `|| 2` and `if (level)`
  lose it. Grep before shipping anything level-aware.
- **Seed the RNG through a mixer.** Small seeds in tests must be as
  fair as big seeds in production.
- **Rotate by rounds finished, not rows written** — a wrong tap must
  not skip a child ahead.
- **The robot's painting is a trace too.** Wipe storage and the
  gallery, not just attempt rows.
