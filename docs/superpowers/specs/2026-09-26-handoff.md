## 6. Rules added

- **0 is falsy.** `?? 2` keeps a Level 0; `|| 2` and `if (level)`
  lose it. Grep before shipping anything level-aware.
- **Seed the RNG through a mixer.** Small seeds in tests must be as
  fair as big seeds in production.
- **Rotate by rounds finished, not rows written** — a wrong tap must
  not skip a child ahead.
- **The robot's painting is a trace too.** Wipe storage and the
  gallery, not just attempt rows. Same for `garden.morning` after a
  gate walk — or a child skips her real checklist that day.
- **A migration must never re-add a constraint a later one widens.**
  Tested now.
- **Playwright `text=done` matches "have you done?"** — use
  `getByRole('button', { name, exact: true })`.
