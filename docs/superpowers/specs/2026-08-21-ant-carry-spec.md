# The Ant Colony Carries — spec

*2026-08-21. Second of the three math animals from the owner
brainstorm (crow = tables, ants = multi-digit addition, hummingbird =
fluency). The owner's brief: complex addition, 3- and 4-digit
numbers, with a strong visual mnemonic — "more than exercises."*

## The mnemonic IS the vocabulary

Column addition's core move is called CARRYING, and ants carry fifty
times their own weight, in columns, as a team. This is not a theme
painted on; the animation and the algorithm are the same event:

- A column sum is drawn as ant-work: each column is a lane, each
  one-ant carries a single seed.
- When ten seeds pile up in the ones lane, ten ants BUNDLE them into
  one big bundle, and one strong ant **carries it over the lane wall**
  into the tens lane. The "+1" the child writes at the top of the
  next column is that ant, drawn arriving.
- Nothing is ever carried except a completed ten — the animation
  cannot show the algorithm being done wrong.

## Tools

1. **WATCH** — the colony performs one sum; the carry is narrated:
   "ten seeds is one bundle — carry it over."
2. **HELP THE CARRIER** — she does the bundling: taps ten ones to
   bundle them, drags the bundle ant over the wall. The digit writes
   itself only when the bundle lands — the motion is the math.
3. **REAL SUMS** — standard practice items from the skills above,
   with the ant lanes available as a hint rendering of the current
   problem (same shape as the crow's picture hints).

## Rules

No timers. No coins (the anti-farming ledger gains nothing). Misses
cost nothing and summon the ants. The carry animation must be
proof-rendered with a named check: ten distinct seeds, one bundle,
one carrier, the wall visibly crossed.

## Out of scope

Subtraction's "borrowing" (the ants taking a bundle BACK over the
wall) is the natural sequel and deliberately phase 2 — one direction
at a time.
