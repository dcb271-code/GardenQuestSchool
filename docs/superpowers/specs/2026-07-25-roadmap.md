# Roadmap — July 2026

Written 2026-07-25, after a week that added a lot of surface area:
Beyond the Trellis, the Level-3 track, the Japanese garden and writing
table, and the music room.

The ordering principle here is **confidence before content**. A large
amount of new work shipped without ever being used on the actual
device, and the one piece of real-world feedback we got — Cecily
couldn't reliably tap the kana choices — was a bug I had explicitly
flagged as unverified and shipped anyway. That is the signal to act
on.

---

## Phase 0 — Find out what's actually broken

Everything below this line is guesswork until these two are done.

### 0.1 A device pass with Cecily *(highest value, lowest effort)*

Nothing shipped since 2026-07-19 has been used on the Cozyla. Verified
by tests and rendered stills only:

| Feature | What to watch |
|---|---|
| Beyond the Trellis | portrait panning, the trellis sign, walking sisters |
| Researcher quests | modal length on a short screen |
| Estimation duel | the SVG scenes, the reveal panel, scrolling |
| Japanese writing table | tracing accuracy, whether the audio actually fires |
| Music room | echo keyboard at portrait width, tap-back timing |

The kana grid proves the class of bug that survives tests: **touch
target and layout problems are invisible to everything except a
finger.** Expect to find two or three more.

### 0.2 Check the numbers against the gates

Never done, promised twice. The beyond-the-trellis quadrants open at
1400/1600/1800/2000 lifetime correct answers, and the japanese bed at
950–1300. Those numbers were picked from the shape of the existing
ladder, **not** from Cecily's actual count.

If she is at 700, an entire garden is theoretical for months and the
tuning needs another pass. Two ways to answer it, and the second is
better:

- a one-off query against prod
- **surface it on the parent dashboard** so the question answers itself
  every time it's looked at (see 3.1)

## Phase 1 — Close the gaps we knowingly left

### 1.1 Something for Esme

Everything built this week was gated at Level 3+: researcher quests,
the estimation duel, the harder ecology tier. Esme's garden is
materially unchanged. Options, cheapest first:

- a Level 1–2 tier of habitat quests (the content slot already exists)
- the Burrow Bunny's lessons currently grant **nothing** — wiring them
  to a gem is a small change with a real payoff
- a toddler-scaled activity in the grow garden (watering? counting
  petals?)

### 1.2 Rewards for the teaching content

`bunnyLessons` and `forestLessons` are pure teaching with no
completion reward at any level. The file header notes mastery-gated
lessons were *intended and never built*. Both a Level 4–5 band and a
gem on completion are straightforward.

### 1.3 Rare species beyond the first three

Researcher badges currently unlock exactly three creatures. Once all
six habitats are badged there is nothing left to earn, which blunts
the loop just as it gets going.

## Phase 2 — Depth in what's already loved

### 2.1 Music (Cecily is furthest along here)

In pedagogical order, and all of it demonstrable with the existing
synth:

1. **Solfège, movable-do** — the strongest single idea. Kodály's
   approach teaches pitch *relationships* rather than absolute notes,
   and the hand signs put interval size into the body. Pairs neatly
   with the tracing pad built for Japanese: she could draw the hand
   shape.
2. **Dynamics, articulation, tempo** — p/mf/f, staccato vs legato,
   andante/allegro/ritardando. Cheap, because *hearing the difference
   is the lesson*.
3. **Chords: I and V7** — "does this sound finished?"
4. **Major vs minor** — reliably a favourite.
5. **Key signatures**, then **the C major scale with thumb-under**.
6. **Compose a song for the garden** — Orff-style; a pentatonic set
   means anything she writes sounds good.
7. **A teacher-steering surface** so the topics her lesson covers this
   month can be pushed to the front. Explicitly requested.

### 2.2 Japanese

1. The remaining five hiragana rows (は ま や ら わ), then dakuten
   (が ざ だ ば), then katakana.
2. **KanjiVG** for real stroke paths. The current guides are
   hand-authored approximations carrying stroke count, order and
   direction only; the intricate kanji (桜 菊 藤 苔) are excluded
   rather than teach a guessed shape. KanjiVG would let them in with
   authentic strokes.
3. **Haiku** — 5-7-5 is literally syllable segmentation, a reading
   skill the app already teaches. Would be the first activity uniting
   reading practice with the garden.

### 2.3 Garden

1. **Three Friends of Winter** — adding pine 松 and ume 梅 completes a
   real cultural set with the bamboo already there, and the kanji
   layer exists to carry it.
2. **Food-web builder** — still the richest unbuilt idea: drag species
   into who-eats-whom chains, discover that removing one breaks it.
   The 17-species catalog already has the habitat links it needs.
3. **Seasons** — `season.ts` exists but the grow garden ignores it.
   The japanese bed is where seasonality is culturally real (hanami,
   momijigari, winter camellia).

## Phase 3 — Make it maintainable

### 3.1 Parent dashboard

There is no single place to see: lifetime correct per learner, how far
each is from the next gate, which units are due for review, what's
been mastered. This would have prevented Phase 0.2 from being an open
question at all.

### 3.2 Housekeeping

- `components/child/garden/LockedGate.tsx` is dead code (nothing
  imports it) and actively misleading — it made me believe the branch
  gates were emoji when they are fully drawn.
- The full vitest suite hangs under worker parallelism;
  `--no-file-parallelism` runs 622 tests in ~15s. Worth pinning in the
  config so nobody else loses twenty minutes to it.
- Music tap-back timing tolerance (45% per gap, floor 280ms) is a
  guess and should be tuned against a real seven-year-old.

  both branch maps, all three characters. Guarded by
  `tests/components/branchArtCoverage.test.tsx`.
- New subjects until the existing ones are verified on-device. The
  pattern for adding one is settled (music proved it: attempt rows
  with a null `item_id` feed the garden with no engine changes), so
  there's no need to prove it again.
